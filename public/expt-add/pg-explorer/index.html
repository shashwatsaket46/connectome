A solve is one (fold, seed, split) run. "Same" means the solve's on/off calls (P·G > 0.5) match the majority over all solves on at least the threshold share of the region cells it covers. Cells take the type their metacell is assigned to: the type with the largest summed P over all solves (average), or the type that wins most of the 50 fold × seed runs (majority).

Anatomy: where the model fits

Held-out fit drawn on the real neurons. Every type is scored on the test block of the fold that held it out (10 folds × 5 seeds): r = Pearson r between observed C and predicted Ĉ over the type's row and column there, loss = their mean squared residual after one rescale of Ĉ per solve. Voxels average r (or loss) over the types whose arbors pass through them.
